## Steps to reproduce

1. Book a table with phone `98765432101` (11 digits).
2. Submit.

**Test data:** phone = 98765432101 and 987654321012

## Expected result

Rejected – phone must be exactly 10 digits.

## Actual result

Booking accepted and stored with the 11/12-digit number.
